# Privacy Policy — Paramanu Seniors Notices

**Last updated: 2 September 2026**

This policy describes how the Android app **Paramanu Seniors Notices**
(`org.paramanuseniorshealth.notices`) handles information. The app is published on behalf of
Paramanu Seniors Health, which is responsible for the notices it delivers.

## In short

Paramanu Seniors Notices does not ask you for your name, telephone number, email address, postal address,
date of birth, or any health information, and it does not collect any of them. It has no
advertising, no analytics, and no tracking. The notices it delivers are the same public
announcements published on paramanuseniorshealth.org.

## What the app stores on your phone

- The notices you have received, so you can read one again after dismissing it.
- Pictures and notice documents that came with them. Only the most recent are kept; older ones are
  deleted automatically.
- Your activation code, and which kinds of notice you have chosen to receive.

This information stays on your phone. It is not uploaded. Using **Reset this app** in Settings, or
uninstalling the app, removes all of it.

## What the app sends over the internet

**An anonymous account identifier.** When you enter your activation code, the app creates an
anonymous account with Google Firebase. This is a random identifier. It is not connected to your
name, your telephone number, your email address, or any account you may have with Google. The
identifier is stored alongside your activation code so that the organisation can confirm the code
has been used, and can stop notices to a particular phone if asked.

**Your activation code**, for the same purpose.

**A message registration for your app installation.** Google Firebase Cloud Messaging assigns an
identifier to this installation of the app so notices can be delivered to it. The app itself does
not read or store this identifier. Google's handling of it is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

**Ordinary connection information.** As with any app or website, your device's internet address is
visible to the servers it connects to while a notice is being fetched.

Nothing else is sent. In particular, the app does not send your location, your contacts, your
photographs, an advertising identifier, or any record of what you read.

## What the app does not do

- No advertising, and no advertising identifier. The app does not request the Android advertising
  ID permission.
- No analytics or usage tracking of individuals.
- No sale or sharing of information with anyone for marketing.
- No medical advice, diagnosis, prescriptions, or appointment booking. The app delivers
  announcements only, and is not a substitute for contacting the dispensary or your doctor.

## Who processes this information

Google, through the Firebase service, on behalf of Paramanu Seniors Health. Messages are delivered
by Firebase Cloud Messaging; the activation record is held in a Firebase database located in
Singapore. Google's practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy at
https://policies.google.com/privacy and Firebase's data handling at
https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

## How long it is kept

Notices and pictures on your phone remain until you delete them, reset the app, or uninstall it.

The record connecting your activation code to its anonymous identifier is kept while the code is in
use. If you ask the organisation to release your code, or it is withdrawn, that record is removed.

## Your choices

- **Choose what you receive.** Settings lets you turn each kind of notice on or off.
- **Stop everything.** Turn off notifications for the app in Android Settings, or uninstall it.
- **Remove your information.** **Reset this app** in Settings deletes your code and every saved
  notice from your phone. To have the activation record removed as well, contact the organisation
  using the details below.

## Children

Paramanu Seniors Notices is intended for adults. It is not directed at children and is not designed to
appeal to them.
