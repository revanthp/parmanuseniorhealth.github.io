# Privacy Policy — Paramanu Seniors Notices

**Last updated: 16 October 2026**

This policy describes how the Android app **Paramanu Seniors Notices**
(`org.paramanuseniorshealth.notices`) handles information. The app is published on behalf of
Paramanu Seniors Health, which is responsible for the notices it delivers.

## In short

- The app never asks you for your name, telephone number, email address, postal address, date of
  birth, or any health information.
- No advertising, and no advertising identifier. Nothing the app collects is sold or shared for
  marketing.
- There is no account and no password. Your phone is identified only by your activation code and
  by random identifiers created for this installation of the app — never by your name.
- Notices, and the pictures and circulars that come with them, stay on your phone. They are not
  uploaded anywhere.
- When measurement is switched on, what it records is tied to a random identifier for this
  installation, never to your name, your telephone number or your activation code. These
  measurements are viewed only in aggregate — as combined totals and averages across all phones.
  [What the app measures](#what-the-app-measures) below lists exactly what is recorded.
- Your name, CHSS number and telephone number are written down at the counter only if you ask for
  them to be kept. See [Details recorded at the counter](#details-recorded-at-the-counter).

The notices the app delivers are the same public announcements published on
paramanuseniorshealth.org. Activation codes are given only to CHSS beneficiaries registered under
Vashi Dispensary; before giving you a code, the HelpDesk or a registered volunteer may check your
name, CHSS number and telephone number.

## What the app stores on your phone

- The notices you have received, so you can read them again whenever you want. They stay until you
  delete them.
- Pictures and circulars that came with the notices. To save space, the oldest of these may be
  cleared from your phone; the notice itself stays, and the picture or circular can be downloaded
  again with a tap.
- Your activation code, and which kinds of notice you have chosen to receive.

This information stays on your phone and is not uploaded. Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

## What the app sends over the internet

**An anonymous account identifier.** When you enter your activation code, the app creates an
anonymous account with Google Firebase. This is a random identifier. It is not connected to your
name, your telephone number, your email address, or any account you may have with Google. It is
stored alongside your activation code so that the organisation can confirm the code has been used,
and can stop notices to a particular phone if asked.

**Your activation code**, for the same purpose.

**A message registration for your app installation.** Google Firebase Cloud Messaging assigns an
identifier to this installation of the app so notices can be delivered to it. The app itself does
not read or store this identifier. Google's handling of it is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

**Ordinary connection information.** As with any app or website, your device's internet address is
visible to the servers it connects to: Google Firebase, and the website a picture or circular is
downloaded from, usually paramanuseniorshealth.org.

Apart from the usage measurements, and the records of whether a notice reached your phone, both
described under [What the app measures](#what-the-app-measures), nothing else is sent. In particular, the app does not ask for permission to see your precise location, and
does not send your contacts, your photographs, or an advertising identifier.

## Details recorded at the counter

This part is optional, and happens only if you ask for it.

If you ask, the HelpDesk can record your **name**, **CHSS number** and **telephone number** against
your activation code. This lets the HelpDesk find your code again if you lose your slip, change your
phone, or call for help.

- These details are entered by the HelpDesk, not by the app. They are never sent to any phone,
  including yours.
- Only HelpDesk staff and volunteers named by Paramanu Seniors Health can see them.
- They are held in the same Firebase database as the activation record.
- You can ask for them to be corrected or removed at any time. Once removed, they are erased
  permanently within 60 days.

## What the app measures

To find out whether the app is working properly on real phones, the organisation may turn on
temporary measurement of how the app is used. Measurement is not collected all the time.

When measurement is active, the app records what happens as it is used, and sends those records
to Google Analytics for Firebase. Separately, Google's messaging service records whether a notice
reached your phone, which happens when the notice is delivered rather than when you use the app.
All of these records are tied to a random identifier created for this installation of the app. They
are not tied to your name, your telephone number, your email address, your activation code, or any
Google account.

The organisation reviews these measurements only in aggregate — as combined numbers, averages, and totals
across all phones (for example, how long pictures take to download on average, or what percentage of
notices are opened from notifications) — never as an individual log of your personal activity.

What is recorded when measurement is active:

- That a notice arrived, and that the app was opened — including whether you opened it by
  tapping the notification or from your home screen.
- **Whether a notice reached your phone at all.** Google's messaging service records that a notice
  was accepted for sending and, when this is switched on, that it arrived on this installation,
  with the time, and the reason if it did not arrive — for example that the phone was switched off
  or had no connection. This happens when the notice is delivered, whether or not you ever open the
  app. It records that a notice arrived, never the words of it.
- Which notices were on screen and for how long, and when a picture or document was opened.
  Notices are recorded by their reference number only. The words of a notice are never sent.
- How long pictures and documents took to arrive, how large they were, and whether they
  failed.
- Whether the phone was on wifi or mobile data at the time, and whether it was roaming. The
  app does not record which network you are on, or your telephone number.
- What happened when an activation code was entered: whether it was accepted, and if not, the
  reason. The code itself is sent only for the purpose described above, never as part of
  these measurements.
- If the app stops unexpectedly, a technical report describing what went wrong. This goes to
  Google Crashlytics rather than Google Analytics. Unlike the measurements above, this report does
  include the phone's model and its state at the time, because that is usually what explains the
  fault. **Crash reports are the one thing that is always on**, even when measurement is switched
  off: they record that the app broke, never what you were reading. See
  [Your choices](#your-choices).
- **Standard properties attached by Google.** The app does not ask for permission to access your
  device location or hardware identifiers. Google's measurement service still attaches a few
  standard properties to the records it receives: the Android version, the app version, your
  language setting, and the country your internet connection is in. The organisation has switched
  off Google's collection of more precise location and device details, so the city you are in, and
  your phone's make and model, are not recorded with these measurements.

This is a list of the main things recorded when measurement is on. What the app itself never records:
your contacts, your photographs, an advertising identifier, health records, the text of any notice, or anything you type other than whether an activation code was accepted.

## What the app does not do

- No advertising, and no advertising identifier. The app does not request the Android advertising
  ID permission.
- No tracking of you as a person. Any usage measurements collected when measurement is turned on
  are viewed only in aggregate across all users and are tied to a random identifier for this installation,
  not to you. The app never gives the measurements a user identifier of any kind, never includes your
  activation code in them, and records notices only by their reference number. So the measurements
  contain nothing that links them to the activation records or counter details.
- No sale or sharing of information with anyone for marketing.
- No health or medical information collection. The app does not request or store medical records,
  health data, prescriptions, or appointment bookings. It delivers announcements and public notices only,
  and is not a substitute for contacting the dispensary or your doctor.

## Who processes this information

Google, through the Firebase service, on behalf of Paramanu Seniors Health (the data controller). Messages are delivered
by Firebase Cloud Messaging; the activation record, and any details recorded at the counter, are held in a Firebase database located in Singapore; usage measurements (when active) are processed by Google Analytics for Firebase and stored in Google BigQuery in Mumbai, India; records of whether a notice reached a phone are produced by Firebase Cloud Messaging and stored in the same Google BigQuery in Mumbai, India; technical reports about unexpected stops are processed by Google Crashlytics, which holds them on Google's own servers. Google's practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy at
https://policies.google.com/privacy and Firebase's data handling at
https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

## How long it is kept

Notices on your phone are kept until you delete them or uninstall the app.

The record connecting your activation code to its anonymous identifier is kept while the code is in
use. Uninstalling the app does not remove it. If you ask the organisation to release your code, or
the code is withdrawn, that record is removed.

Details recorded at the counter are kept until you ask for them to be removed, or until you are
removed from the notices list. They are then erased permanently within 60 days.

Usage measurements (when collected) are kept by Google Analytics for two months, which is the shortest period
it allows. The detailed records used for analysis are deleted automatically after sixty days.
Technical reports about unexpected stops are kept for ninety days.

## Your choices

- **Choose what you receive.** Settings lets you turn each kind of notice on or off.
- **Check that notifications work.** Settings has a button that sends a test notification. It
  appears only on your phone.
- **Delete notices.** Press and hold a notice to select it, then delete it.
- **Stop notices.** Turn off notifications for the app in your phone's settings, or uninstall it.
- **Remove your information.** Uninstalling deletes everything the app stored on your phone. To have
  your activation record, and any details recorded at the counter, removed as well, contact the
  HelpDesk using the details below.
- **Measurement.** Usage measurement is not collected all the time. It is governed by a single
  switch held by the organisation, which applies to every phone, not one at a time. It is turned on
  only when needed to answer particular questions about how the app performs, and the organisation
  turns it off once those questions are answered. The app does not offer a setting for it on your
  phone, and the organisation cannot exclude a single phone from it while measurement is active.
- **Crash reports are separate, and always on.** If the app stops unexpectedly it sends a technical
  report, whether or not measurement is switched on, because an app that nobody is watching has no
  other way to show that it is broken. These reports describe the fault, the phone model and what
  the app was doing — never which notices you read. They are kept for ninety days.
- **Deleting measurements.** Because any measurements collected contain no name or code that
  ties them to you, the organisation cannot pick out one person's records to delete them. What
  does happen is that measurement is off by default and can be stopped for every phone at once, at any time
  (a phone stops the next time the app is opened, and in any case within a few hours — not the same
  instant, and a phone that is never opened does not hear until it is), and any
  collected records expire on their own: Google Analytics keeps them for two months and the detailed
  records are deleted after sixty days. If you want your phone to stop contributing while measurement is active, uninstall
  the app.

## Children

Paramanu Seniors Notices is intended for adults. It is not directed at children and is not designed
to appeal to them.
