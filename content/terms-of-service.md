## What we provide

## Who can use the app

Activation codes are given only to **CHSS beneficiaries registered under Vashi Dispensary**. Codes
are handed out by the HelpDesk or by a registered volunteer.

- **One code belongs to one phone.** When a code is entered on a phone it is linked to that phone
  and cannot be used on another one.
- **If you change or lose your phone**, contact the HelpDesk. They can release your existing code so
  you can use it again. Do not ask for a second code.
- **Do not share your code** with anyone else. It is meant for you.
- We may withdraw a code at any time, for example if it was given in error, if it is being shared,
  or if you ask us to stop sending you notices. If a code is withdrawn, the app will tell you, and
  notices will stop.

The notices are **information**. They are not medical advice, and they are not instructions from a

- **Do not rely on the app alone for anything urgent.** For anything time-sensitive — an

website carries the same announcements, and the HelpDesk can confirm anything you are unsure about.
